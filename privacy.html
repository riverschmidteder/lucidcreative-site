Match! Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Match! is a puzzle game made by Lucid Creative LLC ("we"). The short version: we don't collect anything ourselves. There are no accounts and no analytics. The one exception is the ads on the iPhone version (a small banner and short videos), which are shown by Google and are described below. The Mac version has no ads.

What stays on your device

The game saves your name, your best score and stars for each level, your coins, your achievements, your daily gifts, your progress in each world, and your settings. It also keeps counts of how the game is used, such as which buttons are tapped and which levels are played. These counts stay on your device and are not sent anywhere. All of it is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes everything.

What leaves your device

  • Purchases. Extra worlds and Remove Ads are bought through the App Store with your Apple ID. Apple takes the payment and tells the game that the purchase was made; the game never sees your card or payment details, and we receive nothing about you. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to the purchase.
  • The ads (iPhone only). The small banner at the bottom of the screen, the short video that comes up between levels after several minutes of play, and the video you can choose to watch to carry on a game you lost are ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). The coins you earn in the game are kept only on your device. The first time you open the game, iOS asks whether Match! may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. The game plays the same, and shows the same ads, whether you say yes or no. To show and measure the ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app sends Google information such as your device's internet (IP) address, details about your device and the app, and whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped. If you allow tracking, Google may also use your device's advertising identifier to tailor ads to you and measure them across apps; if you don't, the ads aren't tailored with that identifier. We don't receive any of this about you: Google gives us only totals, such as how often the ads were shown and what they earned. Google describes its practices in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can change your tracking choice any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Tapping an ad opens the advertiser's page in your browser, and that site's own policy applies from then on. A player who buys Remove Ads in the shop is shown none of these ads.
  • Donate (iPhone only). The Donate button opens the charity's own donation page in your browser. You give on that page, under that charity's own privacy policy: the game takes no part in the payment, never sees it and takes no cut. Opening the page is like visiting any website, so the charity's site can see your device's internet (IP) address and the usual details every web request carries. The game sends nothing else, and nothing is charged in the game.
  • Gift reminders (iPhone only). If you ask for them, your phone shows a notification at about ten in the morning when your next daily gift is ready. The phone makes it by itself: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and the game uses no push service. iOS asks for your permission only after you say yes in the game. You can turn the reminders off any time in Settings in the game, or in your iPhone's Settings > Notifications.
  • Report a bug (iPhone only). If you choose Report a bug in Settings, your own mail app opens with a message addressed to us. It holds the version of the game, the version of your phone's system and its model, and the answers you type. You can read and change it, and nothing is sent unless you press Send in your mail app; the game never sends a message by itself.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal information, and we don't collect it ourselves: there are no accounts and no analytics. The only third-party code in the game is Google's ad software on iPhone, described above, and the game asks Apple's tracking question before the ads can use your advertising identifier.

Children

Match! is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. We hold no information ourselves; the ads are described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
