Help with Match! for iPhone

Write to us: river@lucidcreative.associates

Tell us which iPhone you have, which iOS version it runs and what happened. In the game, Settings > Report a bug opens an email with those details already filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your mail app.

I bought something and it is missing

Open the Shop in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Extra worlds and Remove Ads are one-time purchases, so they come back.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles purchases, so Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase. If Apple refunds it, the game takes the item back.

How do I turn off the ads?

Remove Ads in the Shop is a one-time purchase that turns every ad off. To change whether apps may track you across other companies' apps and websites, open your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The game plays the same either way.

How do I stop the daily gift reminder?

In the game, open Settings and switch off Gift reminders. You can also turn off notifications for Match! in your iPhone's Settings > Notifications.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. We never receive it, and deleting the game deletes it. Read the privacy policy for the full list.
